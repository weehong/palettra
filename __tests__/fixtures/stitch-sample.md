## Brand & Style

The brand personality is defined by a high-energy professionality—combining the urgency and passion of a bold crimson with the structured clarity of modern minimalism.

- **Minimalism:** Use generous margins to prevent cognitive overload.
- **Precision:** Use thin, purposeful lines and consistent alignment.

## Typography

This design system uses **Poppins** exclusively to achieve a geometric, friendly, and highly legible appearance.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded** aesthetic (Level 2).
